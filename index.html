Questions
Staying guests can check in (tune in) at 3:00 PM and check out (tune out) at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out can be accommodated subject to availability. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
Entry to the Beach Club is free during most hours with seating available without minimum spend. Between 4:00 PM and 6:30 PM, a cover charge of IDR 200,000 applies (redeemable for selected drinks). Daybeds can be reserved online in advance with a minimum spend, subject to availability.
Yes, we host private gatherings and group events across the Desa. Special group rates start from a minimum of 25 people (with set menus recommended for groups of 10 or more). A 50% initial deposit of total estimated expenses is required to lock in exclusive event bookings.
You can reserve rooms, daybeds, and restaurant tables directly through our website or via phone at +62 361 620 7979. To amend or cancel a room reservation, please email seminyak@potatohead.co (or restaurants.seminyak@potatohead.co for dining reservations).
Following our zero-waste philosophy, single-use plastic bottles are prohibited inside the Desa—walk-in guests can exchange them for a complimentary water token at security check. Parking garages for both cars and scooters are available on-site, along with free unlimited Wi-Fi across the entire property.
